# Historical research standards

Talvisota is intended to be historically grounded. Historical claims and representations should be supported by traceable sources.

For each substantial historical asset or gameplay representation, record:

- The claim or design decision being represented.
- Source title, author/organisation, publication date, and stable URL or archive reference where available.
- Relevant page, chapter, archival identifier, or timestamp.
- Whether the source is primary, scholarly secondary, museum/reference, or informal.
- Confidence level and known uncertainty.
- Any deliberate gameplay adaptation and why it was made.

Do not present an uncertain uniform detail, equipment configuration, location, date, or event as settled fact. Distinguish documented history from artistic interpretation and gameplay abstraction.

Respect copyright and licensing for photographs, maps, recordings, scans, and other reference materials. A historical subject being old does not automatically make every modern photograph, scan, map, or restoration public domain.
