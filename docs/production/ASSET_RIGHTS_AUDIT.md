Every registered record uses `source=project` and `license=project`. The model, material sidecar, texture generator, generated texture maps, and world definitions are original project content; no third-party media, stock imagery, scanned photographs, sound recordings, or externally licensed game assets are currently shipped.

The Sakkola blockout uses the National Land Survey of Finland's pre-war 1:20,000 Sakkola sheet as a research reference only. The raster is not bundled or copied into the repository, and the current terrain heights and precise placement data are hand-authored approximations rather than a georeferenced derivative. The source and attribution notes are recorded in `SACKKOLA_ENVIRONMENT_REFERENCE.md`. The 41 PBR texture maps are generated deterministically from the original `EnvironmentTextureGenerator.cpp` source during CMake builds; they are not third-party textures. VantaEngine has renderer-side tangent-normal and PBR texture sampling paths, but the Sakkola environment has not yet passed real-device visual review and must not be represented as photorealistic or final art.

## Audit result

- All currently registered media and authored definitions have explicit manifest provenance.
- No externally sourced media asset is currently shipped, so there are no outstanding third-party media permission requests for the current manifest.
- Third-party code dependencies have their upstream license notices retained and are identified above.
- A new external asset must not be committed until its source, exact licence/permission basis, attribution requirements, and redistribution rights are reviewed and recorded in the manifest.

This audit is scoped to the current repository tree and manifest. It must be repeated whenever new media or third-party code is added.
