# Worlds

World manifests, region metadata, points of interest, and authored composition. Binary terrain and models belong in assets/.

`taipale.tworld` and `taipale-riverfront.tregion` provide a separate 4 km riverfront environment with a 65×65 heightfield, segmented frozen-water surface, mixed Karelian forest, and three mission landmarks. The `mission.taipale.riverfront-watch` definition selects this world and region without changing the default Sakkola campaign.
